BAHAba Reviewer

Flood early warning and community reports for Odiongan, Romblon

Odiongan only

BAHAba tells residents of each Odiongan barangay when flood risk is rising, lets anyone report flooding, and gives the MDRRMO one verified place to see and act on it.

Problem

Floods are local, but warnings are broad or informal (regional bulletins, social media posts, texts). Residents do not get a clear, official message for their barangay, and the MDRRMO has no structured way to collect and verify what is happening on the ground.

Solution

Rainfall forecasts are checked for every barangay every 30 minutes. High risk triggers an automatic warning. Residents and guests send reports, and the barangay and MDRRMO verify them. One tap calls the MDRRMO or 911.

Scope

Built for the Municipality of Odiongan only: its barangays, its MDRRMO workflow and hotline, and reports limited to the Odiongan area.

30-second pitch

When it rains hard in Odiongan, every barangay is affected differently, but warnings arrive late, broad, or by word of mouth. BAHAba checks rainfall for each barangay every 30 minutes and warns residents automatically when risk turns high. Anyone can send a flood report without an account, and the MDRRMO verifies it before it counts. It is built only for Odiongan, run with the MDRRMO, and it never replaces calling 911 or +63 919 079 8427 in an emergency.

What keeps it exclusive to Odiongan
  • Only Odiongan barangays are loaded (target: all 25).
  • Quick Report rejects locations outside the Odiongan area.
  • The built-in hotline is the Odiongan MDRRMO.
  • Admin accounts are created only by the system administrator.
