# BAHAba Privacy Policy

**Version 2026-10 · Last updated: [DATE]**

This Privacy Policy explains how BAHAba, a flood early-warning and community-reporting service for the Municipality of Odiongan, Romblon, collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information. We follow the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)** and its rules.

**Personal information controller:** MDRRMO Odiongan, Romblon, with the BAHAba development team acting on its behalf.

## 1. What we collect

| Information | Why we collect it |
|---|---|
| **Account details**: name, email address, phone number, barangay, role (resident, barangay admin, MDRRMO) | To create your account, show you information for your barangay, and let administrators contact you about your reports |
| **Password** | Used only to sign you in. It is stored as a secure one-way hash. Neither we nor the administrators can read it |
| **Reports you submit**: description, water level, photo, location (latitude and longitude), time, status, and admin replies | To verify and map flooding and help responders act |
| **Quick Report (no account needed)**: the flood details you enter, your optional name and phone number, an optional photo, and your location if you share it. To limit misuse we also store a **random device ID** and your **network (IP) address**, only in scrambled (hashed) form that cannot be turned back into the original | To let anyone report a flood without an account, limit each device to 3 reports, and let the MDRRMO review reports and block repeated false reports |
| **Device notification token and notification preferences** | To send you push alerts and respect the alert levels you chose |
| **Terms acceptance**: the version you accepted and the time | To keep a record that you agreed to the Terms and this Policy |
| **Approximate technical data** such as IP address and device type, processed by our hosting providers | To keep the service secure and working |
| **Your current location** (only if you tap "Locate me" and allow it) | To show your position on the map. It is used on your device and is not stored in your profile |

We do **not** collect government ID numbers, payment information, contacts, or your location in the background.

## 2. How we use it

- To run BAHAba: sign-in, flood maps, alerts, forecasts, and community reports.
- To send you flood-risk alerts and push notifications.
- To review reports, prevent misuse (such as false reports), and protect other users.
- To improve disaster preparedness, planning, and reporting by the MDRRMO and the Municipality.
- To keep the service secure, and to comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Who can see it

- **Other residents** can see approved community reports (description, water level, photo, barangay and map position). Your phone number and email are **not** shown to other residents.
- **Barangay administrators and the MDRRMO** can see reports, and the contact details needed to respond.
- **Quick Reports** are visible to the MDRRMO. A Quick Report photo is stored privately and is not shown to other residents. Reports approved by the MDRRMO may also be visible to the administrator of the barangay concerned.
- **The system administrator** manages accounts (creating administrator accounts, resetting passwords, changing roles, flagging or deleting accounts on request or for rule violations). These actions are recorded in an audit log.

## 4. Service providers we rely on

We share data only as needed to run BAHAba, with providers who process it for us:

- **Supabase**: database, sign-in, file storage, and backend functions (hosted in Singapore).
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging**: delivery of push notifications to your device.
- **Vercel**: hosting of the BAHAba website.
- **Open-Meteo**: weather forecast data. We send it only barangay coordinates, never your personal information.
- **Map tile providers (such as OpenStreetMap)**: your device requests map images, which reveals your IP address and the area you are viewing.

Some of these providers store data outside the Philippines. We use providers that apply security safeguards. We may also disclose information when required by law or lawful order, or to protect life and safety.

## 4a. Retention

- Account information is kept while your account is active.
- Reports may be kept after approval for disaster-planning and reference.
- Quick Reports are kept for review and disaster-planning. The scrambled device and network identifiers are kept only to enforce the 3-report limit and to block abuse.
- Notification tokens are removed when they stop working or when you sign out or delete your account.
- Audit logs of administrator actions are kept for accountability.
- When your account is deleted, your profile, sign-in record, and notification tokens are deleted. **Reports you submitted may remain in the system in a form that is no longer linked to your account**, because they form part of the flood record that protects other residents.

## 5. How we protect it

Passwords are hashed. Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted by role, with database-level access rules, and sensitive administrative actions can only be performed by the system administrator through a protected server function. No system is completely secure; if a breach that may seriously affect you occurs, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as required by law.

## 6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

- **Be informed** about how your data is processed.
- **Access** the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate information (you can edit most details in your Profile).
- **Object** to processing, or **withdraw consent**, where processing is based on consent.
- **Request deletion or blocking** of your personal information, subject to legal and safety record-keeping needs.
- **Data portability**, where applicable.
- **Be indemnified** for damages from inaccurate, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of your data.
- **File a complaint** with the **National Privacy Commission** (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these rights, contact us (see below). We will respond within a reasonable time.

## 7. Children

BAHAba is intended for residents who can understand and agree to these terms. If you are under 18, please use BAHAba with the guidance of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child's information was provided without consent, contact us and we will remove it.

## 8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. When we make a significant change we will ask you to review and accept the new version in the app.
