# BAHAba Terms of Service

**Version 2026-10 · Last updated: [DATE]**

BAHAba is a flood early-warning and community-reporting service for the Municipality of Odiongan, Romblon, operated with the Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO) of Odiongan ("we", "us"). By creating an account or using BAHAba (the website and the mobile app), you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, please do not use BAHAba.

## 1. What BAHAba is, and what it is not

BAHAba shows flood risk levels, rainfall forecasts, alerts, and community reports for barangays in Odiongan.

- **BAHAba is an information aid. It is not an emergency service.** In an emergency, call **911** or the MDRRMO at **+63 919 079 8427** immediately. Do not wait for the app.
- Flood risk levels and rainfall forecasts are estimates based on weather-model data (Open-Meteo) and on reports from the community and local officials. They can be wrong, late, or unavailable. Do not rely on BAHAba as your only source of warning.
- Alerts may be delayed or not delivered if your device is offline, your notifications are turned off, or the service is down.

## 2. Accounts

- You must provide accurate information (name, email, phone number, barangay) and keep it up to date.
- Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity under your account. Tell us right away if you think someone else has used it.
- Accounts for MDRRMO and barangay administrators are created only by the system administrator for authorized personnel. Residents cannot create or upgrade themselves to administrator accounts.
- One person, one account. Do not impersonate another person or office.

## 3. Community reports and acceptable use

When you submit a report, photo, or message, you agree that you will:

- Report only what you genuinely observed, as accurately as you can, and with the correct location and water level.
- **Not** submit false, misleading, or prank reports. A false flood report can send help to the wrong place, cause panic, and put lives at risk.
- **Not** upload photos or text that are unlawful, obscene, hateful, threatening, harassing, or that show other people in a way that violates their privacy.
- **Not** share other people's personal information.
- **Not** attempt to hack, overload, scrape, or interfere with BAHAba, or to access accounts or data that are not yours.
- **Not** use BAHAba for advertising, political campaigning, or anything unrelated to disaster awareness and response.

### Quick Report (no account)

Anyone can send a **Quick Report** without an account. Each device may send up to **3 Quick Reports**; to keep reporting you need to create an account. The same rules above apply: report only what you really see. The MDRRMO reviews Quick Reports and may approve, reject, or flag a report as **false**. A device that sends a report flagged as false may be **blocked** from sending more Quick Reports. Quick Reports are not monitored 24/7. **For emergencies, call 911 or the MDRRMO at +63 919 079 8427.**

## 4. Review, moderation, and enforcement

- Reports are reviewed by barangay administrators and the MDRRMO. They may approve, reject (with a reason), reply to, edit the status of, or remove reports and alerts.
- If an account breaks these Terms or community guidelines, the administrator may **flag** the account, **suspend** it, **change its role**, **reset its password**, or **delete** it. We act proportionately; serious or repeated violations (such as deliberate false reports) may result in permanent removal and referral to the proper authorities under applicable Philippine law.
- You may contact the MDRRMO if you believe an action was taken by mistake.

## 6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, BAHAba may send push alerts about flood risk in your barangay, including automatic warnings generated when the system detects high risk. You can change which alert levels you receive in your Profile settings or turn notifications off in your device settings.
